# Clozera

Clozera organizes cloze-practice collections for language learners across official, personal, and community-created learning material.

## Language

**Collection**:
A set of cloze-practice sentences that a learner can browse, practice, or add to their dashboard.
_Avoid_: Deck, list

**Practice Session**:
A temporary learner exercise run from a Collection. It presents a small set of cloze-practice sentences for immediate answering without saving learning progress or review history.
_Avoid_: Test, exam, review session

**Collection Group**:
A learning-oriented grouping for official collections within a language pair, such as a track, level family, or topic family. It is not used to express who owns a collection or who can see it.
_Avoid_: Ownership group, visibility group

**Official Collection**:
A Clozera-published collection that learners can view and add to their dashboards but cannot edit. Official collections are maintained through administrative ownership, not learner ownership, and always appear in the Official shelf.
_Avoid_: Owned collection

**Official Content Authorization**:
Permission to create, edit, or delete Clozera-published collections. It is based on administrative authority rather than the learner ownership rules used for My Collections.
_Avoid_: Learner ownership

**Admin**:
A user with authority to manage Clozera-published content.
_Avoid_: Official collection owner

**Collection Shelf**:
A library view section that presents collections by source or relationship to the learner. The primary shelves are Official, My Collections, and Community.
_Avoid_: Collection group, folder

**My Collection**:
A collection created by the learner, shown in the learner's My Collections shelf even when it is public.
_Avoid_: Custom collection, personal collection group

**Community Collection**:
A public collection created by another learner and visible in the library. A learner's own public collections are My Collections, not Community Collections.
_Avoid_: Shared collection

**Collection Creator**:
The learner or admin credited as the source of a collection. Community collections show creator attribution to learners, while private identity details remain hidden.
_Avoid_: Owner label

**Private Collection**:
A learner-created collection that is visible only to its owner and can still be added to that owner's dashboard.
_Avoid_: Hidden community collection

**Collection Visibility**:
Whether a learner-created collection is public or private. New collections default to private, and the creator can choose or later change visibility for their own non-official collections.
_Avoid_: Ownership, shelf

**Collection Relationship**:
The learner-specific relationship to a collection: official, owned, or community. Relationship is separate from visibility and determines which library shelf presents the collection.
_Avoid_: Ownership

**Pinned Collection**:
A collection that the learner has added to their dashboard for quick access. Pinning references the existing collection and does not grant access, create a copy, or make the collection editable; newly created My Collections are pinned automatically.
_Avoid_: Dashboard collection, owned collection, selected collection

**Collection Capability**:
A user-specific permission to perform an action on a collection, such as editing or changing visibility.
_Avoid_: Frontend rule, inferred permission
